## D24 Purchases follow-up council (design only, 2026-11-19; raw/purchases/1_analyst.md, raw/purchases/1_prioritizer.md) (added: purchases extension)

  2. **Day-one enum-only logging in the shared contracts BEFORE the CP0 contracts freeze (Nov 25):** `nothing_subtype` enum (`receipt_order | shipping | booking | meme | other`); guard-block reason counts; `deadline_origin` flag (`printed_return_by | other`); confirm/edit/dismiss rates for flagged cards (F8 extension). Hard rule 3 (no content) holds.
  3. **Eval fixtures:** 10–15 receipt/order screenshots (with and without printed return dates, plus injection-in-receipt cases) added to the ~150-image set; synthetic or own only.

- **POST-BETA queue #1 (merged with receipt → expense):** Purchase action type. Slice 1 = purchase extraction (retailer, order number, items, prices, order/delivery dates, multi-item) + return-window deadline computed ONLY from a hand-curated retailer policy table (anchor order vs delivery date, category/holiday overrides, effective ranges, source URL, `last_verified`, stale flagging; unknown retailer ⇒ ask the user; the model never invents a deadline).
